About Mortice & Tenon

Mortice & Tenon is a small hardware brand that makes wall anchors for hanging things on drywall.

We sell two lines: Reinforced Nylon 66 self-drilling zip anchors and Easy Anchor self-drilling toggle anchors. Both are made in China, packed with screws, and sold on Amazon, which stores and ships every order.

We write our own install steps and print them on every pack. The same steps, with the pack diagrams, are on our install guides page.

Mortice & Tenon is a brand of Class Sales LLC.

Questions, feedback or a bulk order? Email sales@morticeandtenon.us.

A mortice and tenon is a classic woodworking joint: a tenon cut on one piece fits snugly into a mortice cut in the other.
