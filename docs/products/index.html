Zip anchors

Reinforced Nylon 66 Self-Drilling Zip Drywall Anchors

A threaded nylon anchor that drills into drywall with a Phillips screwdriver or drill. Drive it in until it sits flush, then drive the screw through your item into the anchor.

Toggle anchors

Easy Anchor Self-Drilling Toggle Anchors

A self-drilling anchor with a toggle. Drive the anchor flush with the wall, then push the screw through your fixture until it pops forward and the toggle opens behind the wall. Tighten until the screw stops.
