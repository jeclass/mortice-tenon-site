Reinforced Nylon 66

Self-drilling zip drywall anchors

Items required: Screwdriver or drill, #2 Phillips Use on: drywall, hollow walls, sheet rock and plastered walls Hold rating: up to 90 lb in 1/2″ drywall

  1. 1Step 1: Using a Phillips screwdriver or drill bit, press the anchor into your wall

  2. 2Step 2: Apply moderate pressure and screw the anchor into the wall until it’s flush with the drywall

  3. 3Step 3: Insert a screw into the item to hang, then drive that screw into the anchor

  4. 4Step 4: Once the screw is halfway in, the anchor will pop. Continue until the screw is flush with the item

Toggle anchors

Easy Anchor self-drilling toggle anchors

Items required: Screwdriver or drill, #2 Phillips Anchor: 2-1/2 in · Screws: #8 × 2-1/8 in Hold rating: up to 100 lb in 1/2 in drywall

  1. 1Step 1: Hold anchor tip to drywall. Keep the arrow on the head vertical. Press to start. Avoid studs and wires.

  2. 2Step 2: Turn the screwdriver while applying moderate pressure. Stop when the anchor head is flush with the wall. Make sure arrow heads are vertical before next step. Do not over-tighten.

  3. 3Step 3: Place your fixture. Insert the screw through the bracket into the anchor, press forward firmly until the screw “pops” forward about ½ inch, then begin turning—this means the toggle has deployed behind the wall.

  4. 4Step 4: Continue tightening until secure. Do not overtighten. The screw will stop when the toggle is tight. Watch install video on product page as needed.

How to choose a pack

1. Pick the anchor

Comparison of the two anchor lines
Zip anchorsEasy Anchor toggles
Hold rating (1/2 in drywall)Up to 90 lbUp to 100 lb
How it setsThe anchor pops once the screw is halfway inThe screw pops forward and the toggle opens behind the wall
ScrewIncluded#8 × 2-1/8 in, included
Tools#2 Phillips screwdriver or drill#2 Phillips screwdriver or drill
Pack sizes10, 25, 50, 1002, 5, 10, 20

2. Pick the pack size

  • Count your holes. Add up the mounting holes for everything you plan to hang, then add a spare or two.
  • One or two items: the zip 10 pack or the Easy Anchor 2 or 5 pack.
  • A few rooms of projects: the zip 25 or 50 pack, or the Easy Anchor 10 pack.
  • Remodels and jobsites: the zip 100 pack or the Easy Anchor 20 pack.
  • Compare unit prices on Amazon. Each listing shows its current price; divide by the pack count to compare packs.

3. Check the wall

Both hold ratings are for 1/2 in drywall. Avoid studs and wires, and follow the steps above for the anchor you choose.
